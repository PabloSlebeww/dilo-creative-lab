OUR SESSIONS
We host open sessions in Bandung. The address is on Instagram.
TALK #1
with hugo douchet
The History of Design
A talk on how tools (Figma, Penpot, Photoshop, Blender...) influence our creative process. Hugo highlights the biases they introduce without us noticing, and shares practical ways to step back, free our ideas and put the problem back at the centre. The point isn't to throw tools away, but to learn how to put them back in their place.
Get updates
TALK #2
with julien combaz
UX Through Philosophy
A talk that explores UX design from an unusual angle: philosophy. Julien Combaz invites us to question the methods and frameworks we already know well, to better understand what they say, what they imply, and what they sometimes leave out. The point isn't to replace established practices, but to open a complementary perspective that enriches the discipline and keeps us digging, together, into what really shapes it.
Get updates
TALK #3
with salome millet
The Impact of Colour
A talk on colour as an act of resistance and a marker of identity, at a time when everything is becoming uniform. Between pop culture and social issues, Salomé Hélioscope unpacks why colour represents us and why reclaiming it is becoming urgent. Open to all design disciplines, followed by a drink to continue the conversation with the community.
